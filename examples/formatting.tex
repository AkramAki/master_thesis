These examples share the thesis settings and are excluded from the thesis itself.
All numerical values below are illustrative.

\section{Text, citations, and acronyms}
Use \emph{emphasis}, \enquote{language-aware quotation marks}, and citations such as
\autocite{kuypers}. A citation in running text can use \textcite{ma-sebastian}.
Online resources can be cited in the same way \autocite{texmaker}.

The first use of \gls{ml} expands the acronym; later uses print \gls{ml}.
The term \gls{estimator} appears in the glossary.
Use \verb|\glspl{key}| for the plural of a defined term.

\section{Mathematics, numbers, and units}
\label{sec:example-math}
For a vector \(\vect{x} = (x_1, \ldots, x_N)\), the weighted mean is
\begin{equation}
  \bar{x} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i}.
  \label{eq:weighted-mean}
\end{equation}
Refer to it with Equation~\eqref{eq:weighted-mean}.
Inline fractions such as \(\sfrac{1}{2}\) are also supported.
Typeset measurements as \qty{1.23(4)}{\metre}, speeds as
\qty{3.0e8}{\metre\per\second}, and percentages as \qty{95}{\percent}.

\begin{otherlanguage}{ngerman}
Die Zahlenformatierung folgt der Sprache: \num{1234.56} und \qty{1.23(4)}{\metre}.
\end{otherlanguage}

Back in English, the same value is \num{1234.56}.
